MATHEMATICAL MODELS
================================================================================

All exponential distribution formulas correctly implemented:

PDF:        f(x) = λ * exp(-λx)
CDF:        P(X ≤ t) = 1 - exp(-λt)
Survival:   P(X > t) = exp(-λt)
Mean:       E(X) = 1/λ
Variance:   Var(X) = 1/λ²

Lambda Estimation: λ = 1 / mean(response_times)
